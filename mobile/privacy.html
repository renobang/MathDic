Privacy

The MathDic mobile app works without an account. Its glossary and examples are included on your device.

Your saved words, recent terms, and language preference are stored on your device. MathDic does not send this learning data to a server, and this version contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Sharing opens your device’s share sheet. You choose the destination and whether to send the selected term. Pronunciation uses your device’s speech service; voice availability and network use depend on your operating system.

Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Your device’s backup and restore settings may also affect stored data.
